Built for Work

Not a storage unit — a working shop

Built new by Michael Bartlett Construction with the details a working business actually needs.

6″ concrete slab

A full 6-inch slab throughout — park trucks, run lifts, and set heavy equipment without thinking twice.

Real power, your own meter

400-amp service to the building with a separate meter per bay, two 30A/240V receptacles in every bay, LED shop lighting, and 120V outlets on the exterior walls.

Insulated steel building

Closed-cell foam insulation in a charcoal metal building with a metal roof — quieter, tighter, and cheaper to condition than a bare shell.

Climate control available

Add a mini-split heating and cooling system to your bay: +$200/mo on the side bays, +$300/mo on the anchor bay.

Restrooms on the way

Every bay is roughed-in for a half bath. Finished restrooms arrive Spring 2027; serviced interim facilities are on site in the meantime.

Room outside, too

A wide gravel lot with space for work trucks, trailers, and materials — at the end of a quiet road minutes from I-40.

The Layout

55′ × 64′ · 3,520 sq ft

Two 768 sq ft side bays flank the 1,984 sq ft anchor bay. Divider walls are framed between bays, every bay's electrical panel sits on its rear wall, and the anchor bay's 10-foot door is centered under the gable.

Side bays suit a one- or two-truck operation, a workshop, or clean storage with power. The anchor bay fits a small crew, a service business, or light fabrication.
