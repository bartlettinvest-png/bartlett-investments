The Space

Two buildings, phased in

Phase 1 is a 6,000 sq ft building with five bays; Phase 2 adds a second 4,800 sq ft building with four more bays once Phase 1 is mostly leased. Every bay is the same proven layout: a 30′ × 40′ clear-span shop floor with 12-foot walls, its own 10′ × 10′ overhead door with opener, a walk-in door, a half bath, and its own 200-amp service. Combine neighbouring bays and the space opens up — divider walls only go where a tenant split actually needs them.

Why Here

Built for working businesses

Contractors, trades, service companies, and growing shops — space designed around trucks, tools, and inventory, a few minutes from downtown Crossville and I-40.

Real shop specs

12-foot walls, clear-span floors with no posts in your way, a 10′ × 10′ overhead door with opener at every bay, and 200-amp service per bay — enough power for real equipment.

City utilities

City water and city sewer serve every bay’s half bath — no septic to maintain. Gravel drives and aprons throughout, with a turnaround court sized for trucks and box trucks.

Crossville location

On Old Mail Rd in Crossville — minutes to downtown, the Hwy 127 and Peavine Rd corridors, and the I-40 interchanges, with an easy run to Cookeville or Monterey.

The Lot

Two buildings behind 986 Old Mail Rd

The buildings sit on a roughly one-acre lot behind 986 Old Mail Rd, in two rows with a wide gravel drive aisle between them and flat aprons at every door. The site plan goes before the Crossville Planning Commission on December 17, 2026 — this project is in planning and subject to city approval and financing.

Tell us what your business needs — one bay or several, office space or pure shop — and we’ll build the interest list into the plan. Also building in Monterey: flex space on Clarkrange Hwy — the same interest list covers both.
