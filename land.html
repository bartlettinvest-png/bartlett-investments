Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to sell if I reach out?
No. Asking what we'd offer costs you nothing, and you're free to say no or "not now".
Who is actually buying?
Michael Bartlett, through Bartlett Investments LLC, buying for his own account to build on. Michael is a licensed Tennessee real estate agent with Highlands Elite Real Estate, buying for his own investment. When he buys your land he is the buyer, not your agent, and he'll put that in writing.
My land is listed with an agent. Can I still talk to you?
If your land is listed, please have your agent contact us. We won't go around a listing.
What if my land is steep, wooded or has no sewer?
Send it anyway. Plenty of good building land is wooded or on septic. We'll look at it and tell you honestly whether it fits what we build.
Do I need a survey or anything else first?
No. A road name, address or parcel number is enough to start. We'll look up the rest.
