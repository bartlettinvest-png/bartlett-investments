The Space

One building, sized your way

The shell is one open 40′ × 150′ building laid out in five 1,200 sq ft bays. Take one bay, combine bays, or take the whole building at the same rate per foot — divider walls go in only when a tenant split actually needs them, so multi-bay tenants get one open space with no wasted walls. Every bay gets its own 10′ × 10′ overhead door, walk-in door, and half bath.

Why Here

Built for working businesses

Contractors, trades, service companies, and growing shops — space designed around trucks, tools, and inventory, on a highway lot with room to move.

Sizes to fit

The building starts as one open space. Partitions land on 30-foot bay lines only when a tenant split calls for them — take multiple bays and there's nothing between you and the full floor.

Highway frontage

Frontage on Clarkrange Hwy (Hwy 62) between Monterey and Clarkrange, minutes from the I-40 interchange at Monterey and an easy run to Cookeville or Crossville.

Room to work outside

A 2-acre lot with a wide gravel apron, parking at your door, and open ground behind the building — space for trailers, materials, and equipment.

The Lot

2.08 acres on Hwy 62

The building sits at the highway end of a deep 2-acre lot, with the gravel drive and apron along the east side and open ground behind it. Septic is already permitted for the building, and power and water are at the lot.
